What moved

The suite now finishes in 10m 30s, 15m 25s faster than the 25m 55s baseline (2.47×), and the largest single test JVM shrank from 27.77 GB to 7.39 GB (-20.4 GB). Two changes account for essentially all of it, and neither touches what the tests assert:

  • WhisperNodeTest now runs on the GPU. At 15m 04s it was 58% of the entire baseline suite; it is now 5.5s.
  • The OpenCV Mat leaks on the scene-detection, captioning and frame-sampling paths are closed. Mat is a bare FFM handle with no cleaner, so every decoded frame dropped without close() stayed resident for the life of the JVM. Those forks were the memory high-water marks of the whole reactor — scene-detection alone went 27.77 GB → 1.03 GB. integration-test picked up the same fix second-hand, 12.36 GB → 3.28 GB.

The remaining ceiling is cortex/nodes/facedetect/core at 7.39 GB in a 7.6s module — unchanged by this work and now the single largest fork in the reactor by a factor of two.

Test counts and the failure set are compared below — the point of this run is that the speed and memory came for free, not at the cost of coverage.
